Our story

Started with a mower. Never stopped showing up.

365 Landscaping was founded by Jake Mroz, a young entrepreneur who started out mowing lawns, raking leaves and shoveling snow for his neighbors.

What began as a handful of favors turned into a genuine passion for improving the way properties look across his community, and has since grown into a full-service crew trusted by clients throughout the Pittsburgh suburbs.

"The only landscaping company you'll ever need." The standard we hold ourselves to on every property, every season.
